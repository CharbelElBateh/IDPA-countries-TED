Unsupervised grouping

Cluster countries

Group the 192 UN member states by similarity on a single chosen typed infobox field. Per-leaf-type distance from src/distances.py (Levenshtein for text, log-scale for currency, EMD over taxonomies for distributions, haversine for coordinates).

How it works. Pick exactly one field. Countries missing that field are excluded into a separate outlier bucket. The remaining countries are placed into a pairwise distance matrix, then partitioned by your chosen algorithm.
{# ─────────────────────────────────────────── parameters card ─── #}
Algorithm

Two implementations. k-means partitions around k centroids (the distance matrix is MDS-embedded into Euclidean space first); agglomerative gives a merge tree.

{# k-means knobs #}
k-means knobs

Choose the number of clusters. Lloyd's algorithm with k-means++ seeding, restarted n_init times; the lowest-inertia run wins.

{# agglomerative knobs #}
Linkage knobs

Linkage criterion controls how cluster distances are aggregated during merges. Distance threshold overrides k by cutting the dendrogram at a given height.
